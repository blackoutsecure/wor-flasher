# Code of Conduct

## The short version

This project exists because a college student wanted to run Windows on a Raspberry Pi
and decided to share how. Behave like someone worth sharing with.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy community.

## What we expect

- **Assume good faith.** Most people asking an "obvious" question are not lazy; they
  are new. The Raspberry Pi community is largely how people get started with Linux.
- **Be patient with beginners.** Someone flashing their first SD card is exactly who
  this tool is for.
- **Keep criticism on the code.** "This function does not handle X" is useful.
  "This is garbage" is not.
- **Respect the upstream author.** Botspot wrote and gave away the thing you are using.
  Disagree with a design decision all you like; do so civilly.
- **Accept a "no".** Maintainers get to decide what goes in. Not every patch lands, and
  that is not a personal slight.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission.
- Demanding free support as though it were owed to you. Nobody here is paid.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull
requests and discussions — and also when an individual is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

Conversations that happen on the [Botspot Software Discord][botspot-discord] or the
[WoR project Discord][wor-discord] are governed by those servers' own rules. This
document does not override them.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers of this fork at
[blackoutsecure.app][bos] or by opening a confidential report through
[GitHub's private reporting][report]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of
the reporter.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including a warning, a temporary
ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces.

If the report concerns conduct in the upstream project, please raise it with
[Botspot][upstream] as well; this fork has no authority there.
